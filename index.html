Use of Force

Use of force is heavily enforced. De-escalate properly and calmly at all times.

  1. Aiming your gun at an unarmed civilian or another officer at any time results in an infraction.
  2. Firing when there is no threat results in an infraction.
  3. Shooting or killing a civilian with no apparent threat results in termination from the division.
  4. Mag-dumping a corpse results in an infraction.

Patrol Quota & LOA

Officers must hit weekly shift quota to remain active and eligible for promotion. Quotas are checked Friday evenings.

Senior High Rank+

1 hour

High Rank

1 hour

Middle Rank

2 hours

Low Rank

2 hours

Missed quota Each miss = 1 strike. Miss 3 times in a row without LOA = removal from the division. Always check announcements for current quota.

Promotional Policy

Eligible by hitting weekly quota, consistency, and performance. High Rank+ promotions consider additional factors beyond shift time.

LOA Policy

Request Leave of Absence up to 3 weeks. Request extensions via ticket to HR. Notify HR before LOA ends if you still cannot play — or risk infraction.

Retirement & Reinstatement

When retiring, you may reinstate within two months (1× demotion). If you reinstate after one month, re-training is required. Some certifications are not regained if you are not the required rank.

Punishments List

Follow this list exactly. Deviation is itself an infraction.

Warnings

  • FRP · RDM · VDM
  • Vehicle / uniform regulation violations (one per)
  • Shift farming · Unrealistic avatar
  • Failure to follow chain of command
  • Minor Lack of SPaG · Minor radio/ticket misuse

Strike

  • Ignoring officer instructions (may demote by severity)
  • Shift farming over 3+ hours
  • Major Lack of SPaG · Major radio/ticket misuse
  • UoF violations → Strike → Suspension → Term + Blacklist
  • 3 Warnings → 1 Strike

Suspension

  • Max warnings + strikes · UoF second offence
  • Mishandling a scene · Abuse of power
  • Endangering civilians
  • 3 Strikes = 3-day suspension

Demotion / Termination

  • 1 Suspension + 3 Strikes = Demotion
  • Demotion + 3 Strikes = Termination
  • Murder / Corruption / Harassment / Exploiting → Term + Blacklist
  • Leaving during investigation · Leaving without retirement → Termination
3 Warnings = 1 Strike 3 Strikes = 3d Suspension 1 Susp + 3 Strikes = Demotion Demotion + 3 Strikes = Termination

Commands

Always use the pipe | to separate fields.

!infract (@user) | (punishment) | (reason) !retire (@user) | (message) !deploy (@user) | (@user)

Examples

!infract @xnotzjess | Warning | FRP !retire @xnotzjess | Users rank of retirement | User wished to retire !deploy @xnotzjess | @xnotzjess | (Deployment type)

Deployment requires @Senior high rank team+. Second @user is co-host (or N/A).

Deployments

Cooldown is 2 hours. Only break if requested by Supervisors from the LANCRP Staff Team.
