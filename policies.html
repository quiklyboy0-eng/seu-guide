Patrol Quota & LOA

Officers must hit weekly shift quota to remain active and eligible for promotion. Quotas are checked Friday evenings.

Missed quota Each miss = 1 strike. Miss 3 times in a row without LOA = removal from the division. Always check announcements for current quota.

Promotional Policy

Eligible by hitting weekly quota, consistency, and performance. High Rank+ promotions consider additional factors beyond shift time.

LOA Policy

Request Leave of Absence up to 3 weeks. Request extensions via ticket to HR. Notify HR before LOA ends if you still cannot play — or risk infraction.
