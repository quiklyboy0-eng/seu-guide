Everything you need to know when handling Infractions, Tickets, and related procedures.

Warning LEAKING THIS HANDBOOK WILL RESULT IN AN IMMEDIATE BLACKLIST. This punishment guide must be followed to its exact wording. Anyone not following the punishment list will be infracted.

1. SEU Punishments List

All punishments must be issued exactly as written below. Deviation is itself an infraction.

Warnings

  • FRP (Fail RP)
  • RDM (Random Deathmatch)
  • VDM (Vehicle Deathmatch)
  • Vehicle regulation violations (one per violation)
  • Uniform regulation violations (one per violation)
  • Shift farming
  • Unrealistic avatar
  • Failure to follow the chain of command
  • Minor Lack of SPaG
  • Minor misuse of radio or within tickets

Strike

  • Ignoring officer instructions during active operations — Can lead to a demotion depending on severity
  • Shift farming — Over 3+ hours
  • Major Lack of SPaG
  • Major misuse of radio or within tickets
  • UoF violations — Strike → Suspension → Termination + Blacklist depending on severity
  • 3 Warnings → 1 Strike

Suspension

  • Reaching maximum warnings and strikes
  • UoF Violation — Second offence
  • Mishandling a scene
  • Abuse of Power (may also result in demotion depending on severity)
  • Endangering the civilian (e.g. running in and shooting at a hostage taker while the suspect is actively aiming their weapon)
  • 3 Strikes = 3-day Suspension

Demotions

  • Lying to HR during investigations or questioning → Infraction
  • Bias while infracting → Demotion from IA or any rank above CPT
  • Abuse of authority → Minimum demotion (severity-based)
  • Found unfit for the position (Can be issued to SGT+ by ACOM+)
  • 1 Suspension + 3 Strikes = Demotion

Termination

  • Murder → Termination + Department Blacklist
  • Corruption → Termination + Blacklist
  • Leaving the server during an active investigation → Termination
  • Major harassment or bullying → Termination + Blacklist
  • Exploiting bugs / exploiting RP mechanics → Termination + Blacklist
  • Leaving without retirement → Termination
  • Repeated violations of SEU rules → Termination
  • Demotion + 3 Strikes = Termination

3. Shift Management Information

(This can be appealed, but the appeal must be strong.)

All Speed Enforcement Officers must have their shift enabled while patrolling. This ensures fair shift time is recorded at the end of each week and prevents officers from accumulating time while offline or out of the server.

If you observe an officer with shift enabled but not in-game:

  • Take a screenshot showing date and time.
  • Take a screenshot of their shift status.
  • Write a report in the “Infraction Logs” channel.
  • Issue the infraction before assigning any related role.

Managing Shift Time

To remove, add, or manage someone’s shift time:

  1. Type /shift admin [user] [shift-type]
  2. Click Shift Actions → View Shift List
  3. Copy the shift ID
  4. Return to Shift Actions → Modify Shift
  5. Paste the ID and add or remove time as needed

From Shift Actions you can also delete a specific shift or clear all shifts for a user.

Infraction: Shift Farming = Warning. If the officer’s accumulated time exceeds 3 hours → Strike.

4. Retirement and Reinstatement Policy

When an officer retires through a ticket, complete the following before finalizing:

  • Take a screenshot of their current roles.
  • Document the retirement in the #retirement-logs channel using the pinned format.
  • Paste the policy message below into the ticket so the officer understands the terms.

Required Policy Message

“Before you retire, you need to understand the retirement policy to ensure you are sure about your decision. When retiring, you have up to two months to reinstate. When reinstating, you will be demoted by one rank, and if you reinstate after one month, you will need to go through a re-training. Also keep in mind when retiring, you will not regain some certifications when reinstating if you are not the required rank for the certification.”

5. Deployments and Cooldowns

The cooldown for a deployment is 2 hours.

This cooldown may only be broken if requested by Supervisors from the LANCRP Staff Team. Failure to respect the cooldown will result in an infraction.

6. Commands

All commands below are active and functional. You must use the pipe character | to separate fields. Omitting the pipe will cause the entire argument to be treated as a single field.

Command Syntax

  • !infract (@user) | (punishment) | (reason)
  • !retire (@user) | (message)
  • !deploy (@user) | (@user)

Examples

!infract @xnotzjess | Warning | FRP

Issue a warning for FRP

!retire @xnotzjess | Users rank of retirement | User wished to retire

If no custom message is provided, use “User wished to retire”

!deploy @xnotzjess | @xnotzjess | (Deployment type)

If no co-host, put N/A or leave blank

Deployment command note: You must hold the rank of @Senior high rank team (or higher) to use !deploy. The second @user is for a co-host. The host/co-host fields are automatically filled by the embed — you do not need to type them.
